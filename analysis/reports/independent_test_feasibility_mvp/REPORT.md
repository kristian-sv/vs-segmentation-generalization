## Fixed protection protocol proposed for implementation

1. **Freeze identities and rules first.** Record every development/examined patient identity across all modalities and earlier test analyses, using existing documented aliases. Screen MC-RC2 against that list using available source identifiers, metadata and downloaded-file duplicate checks. Different pseudonym prefixes, shifted dates or unequal hashes cannot prove different people. Investigate any suspicious matches; quarantine unresolved identities. Record the collection's non-overlap statement as supporting evidence and explicitly retain any residual inability to cross-link de-identified patients. Exclude any patient with prior local development exposure, including any MC-RC2 use discovered during provenance checks.
2. **Eligible scan:** declared `T1C`, readable finite scalar 3D image, exactly matched supplied binary nonempty `T1C_seg`, and passed geometry/coverage QC. Missing masks and no-residual postoperative sessions are excluded, never converted into fabricated empty ground truth. An unexpected empty mask is quarantined. Accept labeled postoperative residuals and post-SRS cases; retain treatment categories, including unknown, for interpretation. No tumor-volume, scanner, performance or new resolution cutoff. Record anisotropy and reconcile the thickness flags rather than trusting nominal collection limits.
3. **One scan per patient:** choose the earliest eligible shifted imaging date; break same-date ties lexicographically by full source filename. If an earlier scan fails a fixed technical criterion, log the reason before considering the next. Check source image/mask dimensions, voxel sizes, units, qform/sform consistency, orientation, affine/world-coordinate agreement and tumor coverage. Require agreement within 0.001 mm for grids claimed identical; otherwise use documented world-coordinate resampling and nearest-neighbor masks, never affine reassignment. Inspect image–label overlays without predictions; ensure foreground survives orientation, resampling and cropping. Unresolved pairing, geometry or truncation fails eligibility.
4. **Separate pilot and final cohort.** Sort metadata candidate patient IDs by ascending SHA-256 of UTF-8 `independent-t1-mvp-v1|<Patient_ID>`. Reserve the first three entire patients permanently for technical work, including all their sessions. From the remaining order, take the first **50 patients passing eligibility**, or all if fewer. Require at least **30** for this optional MVP; this is a workload/utility gate, not a power calculation or evidence of precise superiority. The 534 scans/185 patients above are only a metadata ceiling. Log all exclusions and freeze final patient IDs, filenames, checksums and rules before scoring; no replacement after results are seen.
5. **Complete model selection before opening final results.** Select the final model using existing development/validation evidence only. By the end of week 3, freeze checkpoint hash, code/configuration, preprocessing, inference/postprocessing and metric definitions, including failure handling and patient-level uncertainty summaries. Pilot work is for technical correctness, without comparative scores or candidate-driven tuning. Keep final predictions and metrics unopened until both cohort and model freezes are recorded; evaluate in week 4 and write in week 5. Failed predictions stay in the report under the frozen failure policy. Any subsequent tuning makes this cohort retrospective too; disclose that rather than restoring an untouched-test claim.

**Proposed preparation:** a separate VS-MC-RC2 inventory and three-patient technical pilot, without running predictions, recording access, archive/file checksums, image–mask pairing, development-identity screening, exclusions, geometry checks, pilot reservation and a frozen proposed final manifest. Unresolved access, identity evidence or systematic mask/geometry defects trigger the fallback; do not spend thesis weeks waiting for support or new annotations. **Realistic fallback:** retain the existing **22-case analysis as retrospective external evidence with documented patient-overlap exclusions**. Its results have already been examined, so it cannot support untouched-test, unbiased final-selection or fresh confirmatory claims. Keep that distinction in the thesis; do not present a newly released scan of a familiar patient as independent evidence. The original MC-RC v2 release currently supplies no verified labeled-T1 alternative, so it does not change this fallback.

**If extra weeks become available:** an additional **2–3 weeks** would make fuller MC-RC2 coverage, identity/count reconciliation with the providers, and treatment-stratified analysis more practical. A stronger label-independence check would need blinded expert re-annotation of a prespecified subset; extra time helps only if a qualified annotator is already available. Neither extra weeks nor more longitudinal scans automatically remove annotation lineage or guarantee access. Keep the five-week plan independent of those extensions.
